NOTE: Using two separate cards, one for the system and another for games and userdata, is the preferred setup. Using a single card is possible but not recommended.

# Updating
1. Download the `dedicated-rgds-plus` zip below.
2. Unzip and copy `system.zip` to the root of your TF2/EXT SD card.
3. Eject and insert the card back into the TF2/EXT slot.

# Installing
1. Download the latest [main-rgds-plus][main] image.
2. Flash the image to a small SD card. Insert into the TF1/INT slot.
3. Download the `dedicated-rgds-plus` zip below.
4. Format a larger SD card as FAT32 (MBR not GPT).
5. Unzip the downloaded zip file and copy its contents to the root of the freshly formatted SD card.
6. Add your games and bios to the SD card (the games you won't be playing immediately can go in `/games/archive` to hide them for now). Insert into the TF2/EXT slot.

[main]:https://github.com/dedicated-os/main-rgds-plus/releases/latest
